How to play

  1. Pick a name and characterUse the arrows to change the eyes, mouth and color, or press Rand.
  2. Create a room or join oneShare the 5-character room code with friends.
  3. Draw on your turnChoose a secret word and sketch it. No letters or numbers.
  4. Guess the restType guesses in chat. Faster guesses score more points.
  5. Win the gameThe player with the most points at the end is the winner.

About TeayRub

Everyone takes a turn drawing a secret word. Everyone else races to guess it.

TeayRub is a real-time multiplayer draw-and-guess game. Players join rooms with a short code and play together in the browser.

Drawings that are guessed correctly are saved to the Hall of Fame, and finished games appear on the leaderboard.

Developer note

We made TeayRub for fun as a school project. It follows the simple drawing-and-guessing game design made popular by Skribbl.io, while giving us a chance to create our own version.

Our goal was to build a game that friends and classmates could enjoy while we practiced web design, JavaScript, and real-time multiplayer communication.
